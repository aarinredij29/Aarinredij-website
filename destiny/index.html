The brief

Challenge

A fleet of 80+ vehicles trusted by large corporates, but almost invisible online. The account sat at 200 followers, and decision-makers had no way to see the scale and reliability behind the name.

Insight

In B2B, people trust faces before fleets. Founder-led reels and behind-the-scenes looks at the operation made the company feel real, and gave people something worth sharing.

What I did

  • A 2-month Instagram content plan built around the people and the operation, not just the vehicles
  • Founder reel scripts and shoot plans
  • A refreshed set of brand guidelines, with a branded outro card on every reel
  • Captions for every post
