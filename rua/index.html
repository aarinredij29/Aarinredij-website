The brief

Challenge

The handwork was stunning, but the feed wasn't showing it. Static outfit posts flattened every piece, nobody was sharing them, and the link in bio sat at zero taps.

Insight

The content was strong, but not shareable. A post-by-post audit showed that shared content, led by the block-printing and embroidery process videos, was driving new follows, and that 93% of views already came from non-followers. Based on these findings, we changed the format to shareable, engaging content built to convert that new audience.

What I did

  • Full Instagram and SEO audit, with a client-facing growth report
  • A content-pillar system ranked by real performance, with process reveals as the hero pillar
  • A phased reel calendar of styling, draping and movement reels — every outfit shown the way it's actually worn, not just laid out
  • On-model reels built around motion: the fall of a pre-draped saree, the swing of a drape on a walk, the detail of the embroidery up close
  • "What we cooked / how it looked" — flat-lay to on-model posts that connect the handwork to the finished look
  • A 7-day story series to celebrate the 1K milestone and push a hero outfit
