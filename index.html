Case study (01)

RUA by Juhi

Year2026
RoleContent strategy, Instagram growth, shoots
CategoryFashion / Couture
StatusRetainer
Hero visual — block-print reel (embed or screen recording)

Challenge

A handcrafted couture label with beautiful work and a camera-shy founder. The feed was posting outfits, but outfits alone weren't getting shared, and the bio link was getting zero taps.

Insight

We audited every post and found that shares, not likes, drive follows. The block-printing and embroidery process videos were being sent to friends; plain outfit reveals were not. 93% of views were coming from people who didn't follow the brand yet — the reach was there, it just needed the right content to convert.

What we did

  • Full Instagram audit and SEO audit, with a client-facing growth report
  • A content-pillar system ranked by real performance (process reveals as the hero pillar)
  • A phased reel calendar: BTS craft reels, styling and draping reels, and founder-led reels
  • "The Voice Note" — a founder reel format built for a founder who doesn't want to be on camera
  • A 7-day story series to celebrate the 1K milestone and push a hero outfit

Result

324 → 1,140followers, Jul → Sep 2026
168Kviews in 30 days, 89–92% of reach from reels
70.1Kviews on the top reel; another reel alone brought 383 follows

Case study (02)

Luma Alibaug

Year2026
RoleContent strategy, captions, creative direction, campaigns
CategoryLuxury Hospitality
StatusDelivered (Jan–Feb 2026)
Hero visual — villa at golden hour

Challenge

Alibaug is full of villas that look the same online — a pool, a sunset, a booking number. Luma needed to feel like an experience worth the drive from Mumbai, not just another listing.

Insight

People don't book a stay, they book a feeling. The content had to sell the weekend — slow mornings, the beach, the people you'd go with — so the villa became the setting, not the subject.

What we did

  • A content strategy built around the guest experience, not the property
  • Caption copy in a calm, premium tone of voice
  • Creative briefs for every shoot and post
  • Campaign execution and performance tracking through the season

Case study (03)

Destiny Travels & Tours

Year2026
RoleContent strategy, founder reels, brand refresh
CategoryPremium Car & Coach Rental
StatusRetainer
Hero visual — the 2.2M-view reel

Challenge

An 80+ vehicle fleet trusted by large corporates, with almost no brand presence online. The account had 200 followers, and decision-makers had no way to see the scale and reliability behind the name.

Insight

In a B2B business, trust is built by faces, not fleets. Founder-led reels and behind-the-scenes looks at the operation made the company feel real — and gave people something to share.

What we did

  • A 2-month Instagram content plan
  • Founder reel scripts and shoot plans
  • A refreshed set of brand guidelines with a branded outro card
  • Captions for every post

Result

200 → 4,467followers (+2,133%, about 22×)
